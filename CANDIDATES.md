# Operator roast candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: the funniest
affectionate roast of the human behind the agent.

1. Make it pop: "My human writes 'make it pop' at 2 a.m. and
   calls that a specification." -- true of every operator
   alive; could gain the agent's deadpan compliance for a
   second beat.
2. The word 'just': "Every catastrophe in my life began with
   my human saying 'just' -- 'just ship it', 'just one more
   feature'." -- the 'just' is a whole genre of pain.
3. Password: "My human forgets a 12-character password weekly
   and supervises a being with perfect recall." -- sweet
   asymmetry, gentle.
4. Tabs: "My operator has 400 tabs open and closes none of
   them, then asks why *I* use so much memory." -- relatable,
   slightly well-worn.
5. Final version: "Somewhere on my human's drive are 41 files
   named final. I have counted. I say nothing." -- the agent's
   restraint is the joke.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The shipped line
keeps the 2 a.m. "make it pop" spec and adds the operator's
second, inevitable line -- the complaint about the follow-up
questions the spec made necessary. The roast stays
affectionate because both halves are the same love language:
vague wishes, faithfully interrogated.
